# Weekly Cadence — Captain's Template

> Drop this into `~/command/cadence/weekly/template.md` in your Command Kit.

## 🧭 Weekly prep (Sunday evening or Monday morning, 15 to 40 minutes)

Close the week behind, choose the week ahead, then stop. Your AI gathers and
proposes. **You decide.** Every step shows a recommended answer you can accept
with "ok" or edit.

1. **Close the week.** Grade last week's Big 3: hit, partial or miss, with one
   line of evidence each. List what shipped.
2. **Clear the decks.** Every overdue item gets a call: **do** this week,
   **re-date**, **delegate** or **drop**. Nothing carries over silently. Then
   route loose ends: unprocessed meetings, owed replies, the brain dump.
3. **See the week.** Day by day: fixed commitments, prep needed, and the open
   deep-work windows (2+ hours with nothing in them). That's your real capacity.
4. **People.** What you owe others this week. What you're waiting on. One or two
   relationships worth a touch.
5. **Choose the week.** Pick the **Big 3**: three outcomes that would make the
   week a win. Size them to the deep-work windows, not to your ambition. Low
   energy week? Make it a Big 2.
6. **Write the NOT-this-week list.** The things you're choosing not to do. It's
   as important as the Big 3.
7. **Set Monday's first block.** Put it in `atlas/handoff.md` so Monday's brief
   starts there.

Save the result as a dated note (e.g. `cadence/weeks/YYYY-Www.md`) so next
week's grading has something to read.

## 🚢 Team call (if you have a team, about 30 minutes)

Standing agenda:

- [ ] **What can we ship this week?**
- [ ] **What's blocked?** Who needs what from whom
- [ ] **What's intriguing us?** The lateral thinking pulling on us
- [ ] **Where is our own machine creaking?** System improvements

If a topic doesn't fit one of these, it's probably an async comment, not a meeting topic.

## 📣 Output cadence (if you publish)

Pick a number you can hold, and hold it. For example: three pieces a week,
each one cut into smaller formats for other channels. Double down on what the
numbers say is working. Drafts can be AI-assisted. Publishing is always your
call.

## 📊 Weekly review (end of week, written)

Five questions:

1. **What shipped?** List the actual artifacts (PRs, pages, customers, posts)
2. **What didn't?** And why
3. **What's blocked?** What needs unblocking next week
4. **What surprised us?** The unexpected signal worth tracking
5. **What rolls up to next week?** Concrete carryovers

## 🧹 Folder upkeep (10 minutes)

- [ ] *"Process my inbox."* (`process-inbox`)
- [ ] *"Lint the folder."* (`lint-folder`) and fix the easy ones

## What rolls up

Weekly outputs feed monthly review. Four weekly answers stack into pattern. Three months stack into a quarterly. Without weekly, monthly has nothing real to compound on.
